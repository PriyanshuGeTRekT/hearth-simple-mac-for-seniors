# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Full-screen home with big buttons for people (FaceTime video or audio), apps, websites and a family-photo slideshow
- Reminders shown full screen and read aloud, with I've done it and Remind me in 10 minutes
- Help screen that calls the main helper and explains common phone scams
- Floating Home and Help bar above every other app
- Three text sizes and light, dark and high-contrast themes
- Caregiver settings behind a PIN, optional strict mode, open at login
- Setup files: prepare everything on your own Mac and send it; every entry is validated on import and the PIN never travels
